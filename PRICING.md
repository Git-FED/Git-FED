# Pricing and Support Model

## Current status

Pricing is not finalized in this repository. Do not publish a plan, price, recurring amount, discount, tax statement, or refund promise until it has an owner and review.

## Possible support lanes

### Free public layer

Mission, docs, public project examples, open prompts, and community participation where available.

### One-time support

A visitor can optionally support the work through a verified provider link. The page should explain what support helps fund without promising personal outcomes.

### Recurring support

Subscription support may be offered through a provider-hosted or provider-approved flow. Before launch, publish amount, billing interval, cancellation path, receipt behavior, refund terms, tax treatment, and support contact.

### Future creator economics

If FedPromptly ever hosts a marketplace, document creator ownership, licenses, revenue share, taxes, payouts, moderation, refunds, fraud handling, and termination before accepting transactions.

## Required launch questions

- Who is the merchant of record?
- What exactly is the buyer receiving?
- Which jurisdictions are served or excluded?
- How does a buyer cancel?
- How are refunds and chargebacks handled?
- What happens if a project is discontinued?
- Which support channel is monitored?
