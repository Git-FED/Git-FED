# Support Playbook

## First response

Thank the person, restate the issue, and tell them what will happen next. Do not make them repeat information already in the message.

## Triage categories

- **Access:** page, link, or navigation problem.
- **Build help:** starter project, docs, or deployment question.
- **Project submission:** attribution, status, or showcase correction.
- **Payment:** billing, cancellation, receipt, refund, or provider error.
- **Security:** suspected vulnerability or exposed secret.
- **Safety/moderation:** harassment, abuse, or harmful content.

## Payment response boundaries

Never request or store card numbers, full birth dates, private keys, or passwords in email. Send billing questions to the payment provider's official flow when appropriate and record only the minimum support metadata needed.

## Response template

> Thanks for reporting this. I understand that [problem]. We are checking [scope]. Please do not send passwords or payment details. We will update you by [time/channel].
