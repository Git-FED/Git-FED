# Design Prompts

## Hero visual

```text
Create a 1280×640 social preview for FedPromptly. Use a deep obsidian background, cyan and violet ambient gradients, a luminous network/grid suggesting a digital city under construction, and generous text-safe space. Render only: FEDPROMPTLY; Turn ideas into something real.; A place where people turn ideas into software. Avoid logos from other companies, fake metrics, tiny unreadable copy, and corporate stock imagery.
```

## Ecosystem map

```text
Create a visual ecosystem map with FedPromptly at the center and four clear districts: Fed-EDU / Learn, FedPromptly Tools / Create, Fed Developers / Collaborate, FedPromptly Labs / Experiment. Place future FedOS concepts at the horizon and label them as future. The structure must remain legible at thumbnail size.
```

## Badge system

```text
Create four standalone digital builder-guild badges: Founding Builder, Founding Supporter, Founding Developer, Founding Community Member. Use shared geometry, distinct symbols, dark navy/obsidian, neon cyan, violet, and warm gold. Make them feel collectible and welcoming, not corporate employee badges or military ranks.
```
