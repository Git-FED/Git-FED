FedPromptly Labs

Where ideas are allowed to be unfinished.

Labs is the experimental layer: prototypes, new directions, strange tests, and work that needs a little more evidence before it becomes a product.

What belongs here

Small experiments with a question.

  • A prototype that tests one behavior
  • A visual system or navigation idea
  • A tool that needs early feedback
  • A failed approach with reusable lessons
What does not

Hype without an artifact.

A Lab entry should include something inspectable: a demo, screenshot, repository, write-up, or experiment plan. It should name the question, current status, and next test.

Lab notebook template

Question: What are we trying to learn?

Hypothesis: What do we expect, and why?

Attempt: What did we build or test?

Evidence: What happened?

Next: What will change in the next iteration?

Failure is information.

Archive an idea with care. A clear explanation of what did not work can save another builder a week.
